Training Information

All players are encouraged to attend regular training sessions.

Please arrive early and bring the necessary football equipment.

Players should wear appropriate sports clothing and football boots.

Training Guidelines

  • Arrive at least 10 minutes before training.
  • Bring sufficient drinking water.
  • Follow instructions from the coaches.
  • Maintain good teamwork and sportsmanship.
  • Inform the coach if you cannot attend training.

Train hard. Play together. Represent APU!
